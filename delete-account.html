How to delete your Haven 52 account and the data tied to it.

You can delete your Haven 52 account at any time. Deleting is permanent: there is no grace period and no undo.

1 Delete from inside the app

The quickest way, if you still have the app installed:

  • Open Haven 52.
  • Go to Profile, then Account.
  • Tap Delete account, then confirm.

Your account and the data below are removed straight away.

2 Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, email us and we will delete your account for you:

midnightdeployers@gmail.com

Send the request from the email address linked to your account, or include your in-app player ID so we can find it. Put “Delete my account” in the subject. No reply time is promised, but your account and data are removed once we process the request.

3 What deletion removes

  • Your profile, display name and avatar.
  • Your coins and your coin activity.
  • Your friends, friend requests and blocks.
  • Your push tokens and notification choices.
  • Your progression and public match history tied to you.

Your player ID can no longer be found or signed in to, and your name is scrubbed. The logins are released, so the same Google account or the same device starts a fresh account next time.

4 What is kept, and for how long

Coin and match rows are kept without anything personal left in them, so other players’ own history stays whole. Nothing in them identifies you after deletion.

Guest accounts. A guest account that was never linked lives only on your device. Deleting the app, or clearing its data, removes that account and its progress, and it cannot be recovered.

5 Contact

Questions about deleting your account or data can be sent to:
