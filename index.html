Privacy Policy

Haven 52 ♠ ♥ ♣ ♦

Effective 11 October 2026  ·  Published by Midnight Deployers

Haven 52 is a social card club: you play games like Seep, Bluff, Rung and Joker with friends in private and public rooms.

This policy explains what the app holds about you, what other players can see, what your device is asked for, and how to remove your account. It covers the Haven 52 mobile app and the game service behind it. We wrote every line to match what the app and its server actually do, not from a template.

1 Who we are

Haven 52 is operated by Midnight Deployers (“we”, “us”). For any question about this policy or your data, contact us at midnightdeployers@gmail.com.

2 What your account holds

When you use Haven 52, the service stores:

  • A player ID generated for you. It never changes and it is public: other players find you by it.
  • Your display name and the avatar you chose from the set the app supplies. The app does not upload your own photos.
  • The sign-in behind your account. For a regular build this is a Firebase user identity. If you sign in with Google, your Google account and the email address on it are attached to that identity. A development build instead uses an identifier generated for your device.
  • Your social graph: your friends, the friend requests you have sent or received, and the players you have blocked.
  • Your public matches: the game, the result and when it ended. Private matches are not recorded at all.
  • Your coin activity — every credit and debit — and your purchase records.
  • Your notification choices, and this device’s push token while notifications are turned on.

Haven 52 does not collect your contacts, your photos, your location, your microphone, or any advertising identifier. There is no third-party analytics or advertising in the app.

3 How we use this information

  • To create your account and keep you signed in across your sessions.
  • To run games: seating you at a table, matching you with other players, and recording public results.
  • To power friends, requests, blocking, and your public profile.
  • To keep your coin balance and your purchase history accurate.
  • To send the notifications you turned on, and nothing else by push.
  • To keep the service working and to prevent abuse.

4 What other players can see

  • Your name, your avatar and your level.
  • Your last five public results — win, loss, draw or quit — and your win percentage.
  • Friends can also see whether you are online and whether you are in a game.

Other players never see your coins, the amount you played for, what a match paid you, or your experience points. Those are only ever sent to you.

5 Coins and purchases

Coins are a virtual in-game currency. They have no cash value, cannot be exchanged for money, and are non-refundable once spent. We record your coin credits, debits and purchase history so your balance stays correct.

If you buy coins with real money, the payment is handled by the app store’s billing system (Google Play). We receive a record that a purchase was made, but we never receive or store your card number or full payment details.

6 Permissions the app asks for

The app asks for one permission: notifications. You are asked only at the moment you turn one on, and everything in Haven 52 works if you say no or turn them off later in your system settings.

The app does not ask for your contacts, your photos, your location or your microphone.

7 Services we rely on

Haven 52 uses a small number of providers to run the service. They process data only to provide their function to us:

  • Google Firebase — account sign-in (Firebase Authentication) and push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging).
  • Google Sign-In — when you choose to sign in with a Google account.
  • Google Play billing — when you make a real-money purchase.
  • Our own game server — which holds your account, profile, matches and coin activity described above.

Google’s handling of data it processes is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

8 How long we keep it, and deleting your account

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account from inside the app, under Profile.

Deleting removes your profile, coins, friends, friend requests, blocks, push tokens and progression. Your player ID can no longer be found or signed in to, and your name is scrubbed. The logins are released, so the same Google account or the same device starts a fresh account next time.

Coin and match rows are kept without anything personal left in them, so other players’ own history stays whole. There is no grace period and no undo.

Guest accounts. A guest account lives only on your device. If the app’s data is cleared, or the app is removed, that account and its progress are gone and cannot be recovered. Linking a Google account keeps the account and its progress on the server instead.

9 Children

Haven 52 is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us at midnightdeployers@gmail.com and we will remove it.

10 How we protect your information

Connections between the app and our service are encrypted in transit. We limit the data we collect to what the features above need, and access to it is restricted. No service can promise perfect security, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

11 Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and publish the new version at this address. Significant changes will be made clear in the app.
